1. Install on iPhone, iPad & Mac

Conductor Schedule is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works offline and opens full-screen from your Home Screen or Dock.

  • iPhone & iPad (Safari): Open your schedule link in Safari, tap the Share icon (square with upward arrow), scroll down, and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • Mac (Chrome or Safari): In Chrome, click the Install icon in the right side of the address bar. In Safari, click File → Add to Dock.
  • Checking for App Updates: The current build version (v40) is shown at the top next to the title. Whenever you publish a new version, open ⚙️ Sync & Tools ▾ → 🔄 Check for App Update on each device to refresh the cache.

2. Approved Conductors & Rotation Rules

The scheduler automatically assigns conductors in sequential order for every 2nd, 4th, and 5th Saturday of the month (9:30 – 9:37 AM at the Kingdom Hall). The year selector at the top right advances automatically into future years.

  • Start #1 Anchor Date: Use the Start #1 date selector at the top right of the Approved Conductors panel to set which Saturday Conductor #1 begins the rotation.
  • Yearly Assignment Tally (×): Each conductor card displays a badge (for example, 5×) showing how many active Saturdays they are assigned in the currently selected year.
  • Reordering Conductors: Use the ◀ and ▶ buttons on any conductor card to move them earlier or later in the rotation sequence.
  • Contact Info & Unavailable Dates: Tap the ✏️ Edit button on a conductor card to save their mobile number (📱), email address (✉️), or specific Unavailable Dates (🚫). When a conductor is marked unavailable for a date, the app automatically assigns the next available conductor in order for that single date without shifting anyone else's future dates.

3. Up Next Banner & Reminder Tracking

The highlighted Up Next banner at the top of the schedule always tracks the very next upcoming meeting Saturday.

  • Gentle "Reminder Due" Prompt: When the next scheduled Saturday is within 4 days and hasn't been tagged yet, the banner displays an amber prompt: ⏰ Reminder due (In 3 days).
  • 🔄 Quick Replace: Need a last-minute substitute for the upcoming Saturday? Tap 🔄 Quick Replace right inside the Up Next card and choose the replacement conductor.
  • 💬 Send Reminder: Opens the message composer pre-addressed to the upcoming conductor with their scheduled dates.
  • Automatic Reminder Tagging: Whenever you send a reminder via 💬 Open in Messages, ✉️ Open in Email, 📋 Copy, 📤 Share Sheet, or tap ✓ Tag Sent Today inside the share window, the amber prompt clears and the app saves a green confirmation tag: ✓ Reminder was sent on Nov 16, 2026.
  • Where Tags Appear: The sent-date badge appears simultaneously on the Up Next banner, the conductor's roster card, the Calendar day cell, and the Year List row. Tap the × on any badge (or Clear inside the share popup) to remove it.
Automatic Rollover: Once a scheduled Saturday passes, the Up Next banner automatically advances to the next upcoming meeting Saturday.

4. Sharing Messages & Custom Templates

Tap the green 💬 Message icon on a single conductor card to send their personal schedule, or tap 📢 Share All Schedules in the toolbar to notify the entire group.

  • Short Text vs. Longer Email: Switch between SMS/Messages formatting and full email formatting at the top of the Share popup.
  • Single Group Text vs. 1-by-1 Individual Queue: When sharing all schedules via Short Text, choose 👥 Single Group Text to message everyone in one thread, or 📲 1-by-1 Individual Queue to step through each conductor individually with personalized messages.
  • Quick Contact Editing: Tap Edit Saved Contact above the recipient box when messaging an individual conductor to update their mobile number, email, or unavailable dates directly on top of the Share popup.
  • Dynamic Placeholders: Templates automatically fill in {firstName}, {fullName}, {dates}, {schedules}, and {timeframe}.
  • Customizing Defaults: Edit the wording in the message box and tap 💾 Save as Default to keep your custom wording for future reminders, or ↺ Reset Default to restore the built-in template.

5. Overrides, Trades & Named Cancellations

  • Single-Date Override: On any meeting Saturday in Calendar view, use the dropdown inside the day box to assign a specific conductor. Select Auto (...) at the top of the list to return that date to normal rotation.
  • ⇄ Trade Dates: Tap ⇄ Trade Dates in the toolbar to swap two scheduled Saturdays between two conductors. Yearly assignment counts remain balanced and no other future dates shift.
  • Named Cancellation Reasons: Click Cancel on any meeting Saturday and select a preset reason (Circuit Assembly, Regional Convention, CO Visit, Memorial / Special Event) or enter your own. The reason appears inside the Calendar cell, Year List, copied summaries, and printed sheets while the rotation automatically pauses and resumes on the next meeting Saturday.

6. Cloud Sync & Offline Mode (GitHub Gist)

Cloud Sync keeps your roster, overrides, unavailable dates, custom templates, and reminder tags synchronized across all your devices using a private GitHub Gist with date-level conflict merging and automatic offline queuing.

First-Time Setup (Device 1 — e.g., Mac)

  1. Sign in to GitHub.com, open Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Tokens (classic), and generate a token with only the gist checkbox selected.
  2. In Conductor Schedule, tap the ☁️ Local Only pill at the top (or ⚙️ Sync & Tools ▾ → ☁️ Cloud Sync).
  3. Paste your Personal Access Token, leave Private Gist ID blank, ensure Auto-Sync on Changes & App Open is checked, and tap 💾 Save & Connect.
  4. Tap 📋 Copy Sync Info inside the Cloud Sync popup to copy both your Token and new 32-character Private Gist ID together.

Connecting Additional Devices (iPhone & iPad)

  1. Open Conductor Schedule on your iPhone or iPad and tap the ☁️ Local Only pill at the top.
  2. Paste the copied credentials into either input box—the app automatically fills in both the GitHub Personal Access Token and the Private Gist ID.
  3. Tap 💾 Save & Connect to download your shared cloud schedule onto the new device without overwriting the cloud copy.
Offline Edits & Auto-Reconnect: If you edit the schedule without Wi-Fi or cellular signal, your changes save locally and the top pill displays ☁️ Offline Saved. The moment your device reconnects, the app automatically merges and uploads your changes to GitHub Gist.

7. Print / Save PDF, Calendar (.ics) & JSON Backups

  • Board-Ready Print / Save PDF: Open ⚙️ Sync & Tools ▾ → 🖨️ Print / Save PDF and choose Current Displayed Month, Quarterly Board Sheet (3 Months), or Full Selected Year. This formats a clean, button-free sheet ready for the Kingdom Hall information board or saving as a PDF.
  • Personal Conductor Calendar: Tap the blue 📅 Download .ics icon on any conductor card and choose a timeframe (1, 3, or 6 months, rest of year, or full year) to generate a calendar file with built-in 24-hour and 12-hour reminders.
  • Master Calendar Export: Use 📅 Export .ics ▾ in the toolbar to export the current month or the entire selected year for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.
  • JSON Backup & Restore: Use ⚙️ Sync & Tools ▾ → 💾 Backup Data (JSON) to save a complete offline snapshot file, and 📂 Restore Backup (JSON) to reload it anytime.
