🔒 1. Past-Date History Lock v43

Historical meeting Saturdays prior to today are automatically frozen so that adding a new conductor, reordering the rotation (◀ / ▶), or changing the Start #1 anchor date never rewrites past records.

  • Automatic Snapshotting: Every completed Saturday is locked with the exact brother who served on that date (shown with a 🔒 icon in the Calendar and a 🔒 Locked badge in the Year List).
  • Manual Toggle: Click the 🔒 Past Locked pill in the Approved Conductors header if you ever need to temporarily unlock historical dates (🔓 Past Unlocked) to recalculate past assignments.

✅ 2. One-Tap Confirmation Status v43

Sending or copying a schedule message automatically tags that assignment as Sent. When a brother replies to confirm or asks for a substitute, tap the reminder badge anywhere in the app to cycle its status:

✓ Reminder sent → ✅ Confirmed → ⚠️ Needs Sub
  • Where to Tap: Tap the badge on the Up Next banner, inside the brother's Approved Conductors card, on the Calendar day cell, in the Year List, or at the top of the Share modal.
  • Clear Tag: Tap the small × on the right side of a badge to remove the status tag completely.

📊 3. Last-Served & Fairness Stats v43

Keep assignments balanced when picking a last-minute substitute or reviewing yearly totals.

  • Stats Drawer: Click 📊 Stats in the Approved Conductors header to open a live breakdown showing each brother's Last Served date (with days elapsed), Completed turns, Upcoming turns, and Year Total. The brother who has waited longest is highlighted with a Longest Wait badge.
  • Quick Replace Hints: When you tap 🔄 Quick Replace on the Up Next banner, each option in the dropdown displays when that brother last served and how many times they are scheduled this year.

🚫 4. Date-Range Unavailability Picker v43

When a brother is traveling or unavailable, the rotation automatically skips them on those Saturdays and assigns the next available brother—without shifting everyone else's subsequent dates.

  1. Tap the Edit (✏️) icon on the brother's card in Approved Conductors.
  2. Use Vacation From → Vacation To and tap + Add Range to automatically block every 2nd, 4th, or 5th Saturday inside that window.
  3. Alternatively, use the Quick-Toggle Upcoming Meeting Saturdays checkboxes or add a single date, then tap 💾 Save.

🕒 5. Custom Time & Location v43

Update your standard meeting time and location globally or override individual Saturdays (for example, when meeting at a field service group home instead of the Kingdom Hall).

  • How to Open: Select ⚙️ Sync & Tools ▾ → 🕒 Time & Location, tap ✏️ Edit Time/Loc on the Up Next banner, or tap the 🕒 icon on any future calendar cell.
  • Automatic Propagation: Custom times and locations automatically appear on calendar cells (📍 badge), in text/email messages ({defaultTime} and {defaultLocation}), in .ics calendar invites, and on printed sheets.

↩ 6. One-Tap Undo v43

The toolbar tracks your last 15 schedule changes (dropdown overrides, date trades, cancellations, status updates, and roster edits).

  • Tap ↩ Undo in the main toolbar to immediately revert your most recent change and push the restored schedule to your synced devices.

⇄ Rotation, Date Trades & Cancellations

  • 2nd, 4th & 5th Saturdays: The schedule automatically populates the 2nd, 4th, and occasional 5th Saturdays of every month in strict roster order starting from the Start #1 anchor date.
  • Two-Way Date Trade (⇄ Trade Dates): Swap two brothers' scheduled Saturdays in one step while keeping yearly assignment counts balanced.
  • Named Cancellations: Tap Cancel on a meeting date to pause the rotation for a Circuit Assembly, Regional Convention, CO Visit, or custom event. The next brother in line automatically rolls forward to the next active Saturday.

💬 Messaging, Templates & 1-by-1 Queue

  • Individual Reminders: Tap the green speech-bubble icon on a conductor's card or 💬 Send Reminder on the Up Next banner to generate a pre-filled text or email with preparation references.
  • Group Text vs. 1-by-1 Queue: Tap 📢 Share All Schedules and switch between Single Group Text or 1-by-1 Individual Queue to step through each brother one by one and launch individual SMS threads.
  • Custom Templates: Edit the wording in the message box and tap 💾 Save as Default to store your preferred phrasing across devices.

☁️ Multi-Device Cloud Sync (GitHub Gist)

  1. Open ⚙️ Sync & Tools ▾ → ☁️ Cloud Sync (GitHub Gist).
  2. Paste a GitHub Personal Access Token (classic) with the gist scope and tap 💾 Save & Connect to create your private sync Gist.
  3. Tap 📋 Copy Sync Info and paste it into either field on your iPhone or iPad to pair them.
Conflict-Safe Date Sync: Reminders, confirmation statuses, overrides, cancellations, and custom slot times merge per date so simultaneous edits on two devices never overwrite each other.

🖨️ Board-Ready Print / PDF & .ics Calendars v46

  • Print / Save PDF (4-Column Layout): Navigate the calendar to your desired starting month, then open ⚙️ Sync & Tools ▾ → 🖨️ Print / Save PDF and choose 1 Month, 3 Months (Quarterly), 6 Months (Semi-Annual), or 12 Months (Full Year). The print sheet formats four balanced columns—Date (with 2nd/4th/5th Saturday), Assigned Conductor, Time & Location, and Status/Reminder (Scheduled or Reminder sent on [date])—with generous 0.85in side margins for clean edge clearance on Mac, iPhone, and iPad (on iPhone, tap 📄 Share / Save PDF to print or save directly via the iOS Share Sheet, or tap 🌐 Safari Print to open the schedule in Safari).
  • Calendar Invites (.ics): Download a personal .ics file from any conductor's card (with built-in 24-hour and 12-hour alerts) or export the entire month/year via 📅 Export .ics ▾.
